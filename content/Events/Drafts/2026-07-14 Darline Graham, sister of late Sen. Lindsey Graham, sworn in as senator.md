## Darline Graham, sister of late Sen. Lindsey Graham, sworn in as senator

**Source:** [The Hill — Senate](https://thehill.com/homenews/5967990-darline-nordone-lindsey-graham-senator-oath/)
**Date:** 2026-07-14

Darline Graham Nordone was sworn in as the newest U.S. senator Tuesday, succeeding her late brother, Sen. Lindsey Graham (R-S.C.). It’s the first time in Senate history that a sibling has been sworn in to replace a senator who died in office. Dressed in pale blue, Nordone recited the oath of office at 2:30 p.m.…

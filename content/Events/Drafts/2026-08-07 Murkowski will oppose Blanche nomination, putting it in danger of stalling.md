## Murkowski will oppose Blanche nomination, putting it in danger of stalling

**Source:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/senate/6015924-lisa-murkowski-todd-blanche-attorney-general-nomination-block/)
**Date:** 2026-08-07

Sen. Lisa Murkowski (R-Alaska) on Friday became the second Republican senator to announce she will vote against acting Attorney General Todd Blanche’s nomination to serve in the role full-time, putting his confirmation on a knife’s edge as one more “no” vote could sink or delay it.

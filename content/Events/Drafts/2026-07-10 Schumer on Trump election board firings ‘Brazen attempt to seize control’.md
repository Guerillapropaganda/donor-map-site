## Schumer on Trump election board firings: ‘Brazen attempt to seize control’

**Source:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/senate/5962253-chuck-schumer-donald-trump-election-board-firings/)
**Date:** 2026-07-10

Senate Democratic Leader Chuck Schumer (N.Y.) on Thursday blasted President Trump’s decision to fire members of the bipartisan Election Assistance Commission, accusing the president of making a “brazen attempt to seize control of our elections.” “Donald Trump said Republicans should ‘take over the voting.’ Today, he took another step toward doing exactly that,” Schumer said…

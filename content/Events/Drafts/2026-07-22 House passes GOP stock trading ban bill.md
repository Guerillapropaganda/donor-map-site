## House passes GOP stock trading ban bill

**Source:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/house/5983948-house-passes-stock-trading-bill/)
**Date:** 2026-07-22

The House on Wednesday passed a GOP-backed congressional stock trading ban, overcoming months of delay after conservative hardliners repeatedly pressed House GOP leaders to bring the legislation to the floor for a vote.

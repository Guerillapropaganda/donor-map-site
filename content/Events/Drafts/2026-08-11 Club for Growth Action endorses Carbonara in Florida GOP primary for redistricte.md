## Club for Growth Action endorses Carbonara in Florida GOP primary for redistricted House seat

**Source:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/6021929-club-for-growth-action-carbonara-florida-house-primary/)
**Date:** 2026-08-11

The conservative Club for Growth Action announced Tuesday that it’s endorsing businessman Michael Carbonara in the crowded Republican primary for a newly drawn House seat in Florida, according to plans first shared with The Hill. The new seat, the redrawn 22nd Congressional District, stretches from West Palm Beach to Delray Beach and favors Republicans by 4 percentage points, according to the nonpartisan election handicapper…

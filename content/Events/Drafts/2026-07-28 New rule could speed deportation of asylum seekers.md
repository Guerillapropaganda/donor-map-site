## New rule could speed deportation of asylum seekers

**Source:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/national-security/5994742-trump-administration-cuts-asylum-interviews/)
**Date:** 2026-07-28

Starting Tuesday, the Trump administration will cut off asylum seekers’ access to an interview to weigh their claims, instead allowing officers to funnel migrants into immigration court for deportation proceedings.

## Newsom on whether he’d be a good president: ‘I have no idea’

**Source:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/state-watch/6041621-newsom-unsure-presidential-bid/)
**Date:** 2026-08-20

California Gov. Gavin Newsom (D) said Thursday that he was unsure whether he would make a good president, amid growing speculation he will run for the White House in 2028. Asked during an interview with “Detroit Talk City” if he would be a good president, Newsom responded: “That’s a, that’s a difficult question to answer.”…

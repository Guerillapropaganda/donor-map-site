## State Department issues warning to Americans living and traveling abroad

**Source:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/international/5977134-state-department-travel-warning/)
**Date:** 2026-07-19

On Saturday, the State Department issued a warning to Americans living and traveling abroad, urging them to remain cautious in the face of increased threats on U.S. diplomatic facilities. “Due to heightened tensions in the Middle East, the security environment remains complex with the potential for unforeseen escalation,” the State Department wrote in a statement…

## US Navy restricting press access to sailors’ service history

**Source:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/defense/5986786-navy-stops-releasing-sailor-history-press/)
**Date:** 2026-07-23

The U.S. Navy will no longer release service history information for sailors, ending the decades-long practice to address what officials describe as security threats and harassment concerns. The move comes amid greater press restrictions imposed by Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, which include a requirement for reporters to be escorted by authorized personnel in the Pentagon. …

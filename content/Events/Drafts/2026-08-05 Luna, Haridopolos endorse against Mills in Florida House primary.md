## Luna, Haridopolos endorse against Mills in Florida House primary

**Source:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/6010825-luna-haridopolos-endorsesement-ryan-elijah-over-cory-mills-florida-7th-district/)
**Date:** 2026-08-05

Florida GOP Reps. Anna Paulina Luna and Mike Haridopolos on Wednesday endorsed Ryan Elijah, who is mounting a primary challenge to Rep. Cory Mills (R-Fla.) in the Sunshine State’s 7th Congressional District. In a joint statement, Luna and Haridopolos praised Elijah as a “trusted voice in our community” and a “true patriot and freedom fighter.”…

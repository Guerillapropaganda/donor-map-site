## Washington state governor says he’s talked to Trump about wildfires

**Source:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/state-watch/6005207-washington-state-governor-says-hes-talked-to-trump-about-wildfires/)
**Date:** 2026-08-03

Washington Gov. Bob Ferguson (D) said Sunday he spoke with President Trump and Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin about the wildfires sweeping his state. During his conversation with Trump and Mullin, Ferguson “detailed how dire and devastating the situation is in Spokane and across our state,” the first-term governor wrote on the social platform X.…

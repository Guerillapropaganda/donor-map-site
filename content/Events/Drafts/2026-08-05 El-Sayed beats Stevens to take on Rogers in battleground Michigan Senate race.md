## El-Sayed beats Stevens to take on Rogers in battleground Michigan Senate race

**Source:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/6006337-el-sayed-wins-michigan-senate-primary/)
**Date:** 2026-08-05

Progressive Abdul El-Sayed has defeated Rep. Haley Stevens (D-Mich.) in the fiercely fought Michigan Democratic Senate primary to face off against former Rep. Mike Rogers (R) for the battleground seat in November, according to Decision Desk HQ (DDHQ). DDHQ made its call at 8:35 a.m. EDT on Wednesday in a tighter-than-expected match-up between the pair.…

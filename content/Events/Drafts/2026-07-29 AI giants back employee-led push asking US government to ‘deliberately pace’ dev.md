## AI giants back employee-led push asking US government to ‘deliberately pace’ development

**Source:** [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5996344-ai-development-anthropic-claude-openai-deepmind/)
**Date:** 2026-07-29

Anthropic and OpenAI backed an employee-led initiative on Tuesday asking the U.S. government to “deliberately pace” frontier AI development. More than 1,200 employees have signed on to the statement, which underscored that the world’s leading AI companies could be close to automating AI research and warned that development could move beyond human understanding and control.…
